# Jiayi (Johnny) Li — Personal Portfolio

Personal portfolio website for Jiayi (Johnny) Li, an undergraduate studying Computer Science and Statistics at Northwestern University, with a minor in Data Science. The site works as a living resume for recruiters, researchers, and collaborators to find my technical and academic background in one place.

## What's Here

The site pulls together my research, publications, industry experience, and personal projects. The research section covers work at Harvard Medical School, the University of Toronto, Sunnybrook Hospital, and Northwestern University — building automation tools, data pipelines, and statistical models applied to radiation dosimetry, proteomics, and epidemiology. Publications include two peer-reviewed first-author papers and a co-authored book. Work experience runs from a pharmaceutical chemical analysis internship at Pharmaron Beijing to an AI product management role at a Toronto edtech startup, filterable by role type. Projects include a financial contagion simulator, a full-stack grade-prediction web app (GradeMaster), and a statistical healthcare analysis, filterable by language, with cards linking to GitHub, live demos, and PDF write-ups where available.

The site also includes a skills breakdown across programming languages, frameworks, and data tools; an achievements section covering hockey (OHL Priority Selection, China National Winter Games MVP), academic competitions (Canadian Chemistry Olympiad), and research recognition (AAAS Annual Meeting, CCIR Symposium); and a downloadable resume.
